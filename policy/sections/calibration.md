## Calibration sampling
- For each project's first {{calibration_first_tasks}} full-lane tasks (count the rows in its `docs/model-routing-log.md`), the main session also adds a random {{calibration_sample_percent}}% of Verify: no tasks to the plan's batch verification. Draw with `{{calibration_draw_command}}` and log the draw.
- After those {{calibration_first_tasks}} tasks, read the log. If sampled verifiers found real defects (FIX or REPLAN) in under {{calibration_low_defect_percent}}% of samples, drop sampling to {{calibration_reduced_sample_percent}}%. If they found defects in more than {{calibration_high_defect_percent}}% of samples for a given executor tier or task type, propose that this tier or task type becomes `Verify: batch`, where Logging says proposals go: the project's claude.md, or, where claude.md is generated or not committed, the log's proposals section and the session report.
Count tasks run below their planned tier separately. Once {{calibration_first_tasks}} of them have run with a defect rate under {{calibration_low_defect_percent}}%, propose to the human that they be verified by sampling instead of always; until the human agrees, verify every one.
