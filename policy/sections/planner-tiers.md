## Planner tier selection (made by the main session)
- **planner-sonnet-high:** work confined to one module, with clear evidence and roughly five tasks or fewer.
- **planner-opus-high:** multi-module work; a root cause that is still unclear after scouting; refactors; anything touching authentication, payments, data integrity, or migrations.
- **planner-opus-xhigh:** architecture or system design, large refactors, cross-service changes, or a second REPLAN after a planner-opus-high plan.

Planning is where reasoning effort pays off most, because planners produce few tokens and their mistakes multiply downstream. When unsure, pick the higher planner tier. This is the opposite of the rule for executors.
